We opened the workshop doors on a wet Saturday in June. Forty people came
through, most of them to ask how a slatted seat drains, and two to ask whether
we would repair a bench that had been in the family since 1978.

The next open day is in September. Bring photographs if you want advice on a
repair; leave the bench in the van until we have looked at the pictures.
