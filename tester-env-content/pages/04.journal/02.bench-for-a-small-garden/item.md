A narrow courtyard in Sellwood needed seating that would not crowd the path.
We built a 1.6-metre oak bench with a shallow back and set the legs inboard so
the path stayed clear.

The frame is white oak, the seat slats are cedar, and the whole bench weighs
just under thirty kilos, which means two people can move it without taking it
apart.
