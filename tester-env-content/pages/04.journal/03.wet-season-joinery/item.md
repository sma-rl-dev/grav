October is when we stop oiling and start testing joints. A garden bench lives
through far more water than a kitchen chair, so every frame we build this month
gets a draw-bored mortise and tenon and a bead of marine glue that we can watch
through the winter.

We keep two test frames on the roof of the workshop and measure the movement in
the spring. Last winter the oak moved less than the cedar, but the cedar dried
out faster after a wet week.
